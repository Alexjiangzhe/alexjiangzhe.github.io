About

I’m Zhe Jiang (Alex), a psychology undergraduate at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE). I’m interested in how the brain represents information, and how cognitive states shape the way that information is processed.

In collaborative fMRI projects, I build and check analysis pipelines, from representational similarity analysis and pattern component modelling to feature-based models of neural responses. At ELTE, I work on resting-state EEG preprocessing and quality control in the Music and Language Development Research Group.

I care about analyses that can be inspected: explicit models, traceable decisions and an honest account of what the data support.
